# 0002 Carpools are stricter than rides

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** 2026-09-25

## Context

A carpool means getting into a car with someone, often for an hour.
The ride rules let adult hosts reach friends of friends.

## Options

1. Reuse the ride rules unchanged.
2. Friends only.
3. Friends of friends only when both sides are adults; pickup spot only for
   friends and confirmed riders.

## Decision

Option 3, and no public carpools at all.

## Consequences

A minor sees carpools of confirmed friends only, and their own posts reach
confirmed friends only. The board is emptier for new users; open events
remain the way in without a network.
